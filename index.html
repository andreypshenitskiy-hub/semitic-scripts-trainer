How answers are checked

Your answer is matched sign by sign, so one wrong letter marks only that sign. Signs you get wrong come back more often; signs you read reliably fade out.

Abjads: type every written letter, including vowel letters (w, y, ʾ). Ethiopic: type consonant plus vowel (ä u i a e ə o); the sixth-order ə may be left out. Amharic also accepts the merged sounds (h for ḥ and ḫ, s for ś).

You can type ' for ʾ and ` for ʿ.
